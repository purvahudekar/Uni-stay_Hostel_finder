# Uni-stay_Hostel_finder
**UniStay – Hostel Finder** is a web-based platform designed to help students easily find suitable hostels and accommodation near their college or preferred location.

**Problem:**
Students often face difficulty finding reliable and suitable hostels because information about hostels is scattered across different sources. They may have to visit multiple locations, contact different owners, and compare prices, facilities, and availability manually. This makes the hostel-search process time-consuming and confusing, especially for students moving to a new city.

**Solution:**
UniStay provides a centralized platform where students can browse available hostels, view hostel details such as location, rent, facilities, and images, and find accommodation according to their requirements. Hostel owners can also list their properties and provide relevant information to students. This makes the process of discovering and comparing student accommodation easier and more organized.

**Key Features:**

* Search and explore available hostels
* View hostel details, rent, facilities, and images
* Student and hostel-owner dashboards
* Property listing for hostel owners
* Hostel booking functionality
* Location/map integration
* Database for storing user and property information
